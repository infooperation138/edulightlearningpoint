About Mangalayatan University

Mangalayatan University offers quality Online and Distance Education programs designed for working professionals, fresh students, and learners who wish to continue higher education with flexibility. The university focuses on industry-relevant curriculum, experienced faculty, digital learning support, and student-friendly academic services.

Students can pursue undergraduate and postgraduate programs through an easy admission process while receiving complete academic and admission support throughout their learning journey.
